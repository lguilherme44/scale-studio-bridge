Scale Studio

Scale Studio is a personal desktop tool that produces short educational videos and sends them, at the owner's request, to the owner's own TikTok and YouTube accounts. It runs only on the owner's computer and is not offered to other users.

Ferramenta pessoal que produz vídeos curtos e os envia, a pedido do dono, para as contas dele no TikTok e no YouTube.
